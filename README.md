# BevTrace – Beverage Logistics & Traceability Platform

## Project Overview

**BevTrace** is a SaaS platform that **digitizes and automates the logistics and traceability of beverage distribution**, from the warehouse to the final delivery point.

Our solution integrates **IoT telemetry** from transport units (temperature, humidity, GPS and connectivity) with an automated **rules engine** that detects anomalies, opens incidents and keeps a complete **batch and pallet traceability history**, all in real time.

BevTrace targets two key roles in the beverage supply chain:
- **Logistics Managers**, who plan dispatches, supervise routes and review operational KPIs.
- **Warehouse Operators**, who register inventory, validate cargo and scan pallets.

---

## Key Features

- **Inventory Management:** Product catalog, batch intake by code, shrinkage logging and reconciliation with discrepancy detection.
- **Dispatch Management:** Dispatch scheduling, vehicle assignment with capacity validation, pallet validation and controlled departure.
- **Batch & Pallet Traceability:** Route tracking with checkpoints, delivery or rejection confirmation and a full history per batch.
- **IoT Telemetry:** Connectivity dashboard with critical status after 30 minutes without signal, sensor provisioning and readings monitoring.
- **Incident & Alert Management:** Automatic anomaly detection, configurable rules, corrective actions and notifications.
- **KPI Analytics Dashboard:** OTIF, Fill Rate, ERI, inventory rotation and shrinkage, with CSV report export.
- **Role-Based Access:** Separate experiences for Administrator, Logistics Manager and Warehouse Operator.
- **Internationalization (i18n):** Full support for **English (EN)** and **Spanish (ES)** via `data-i18n` attributes and a language switcher.
